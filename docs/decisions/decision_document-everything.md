- **Type:** `decision`

## The decision

**Document EVERYTHING — requests, rulings, nothing under the radar.** Every request made of an
external channel, every ruling the director gives in-session, every decision that shapes the
work is routed to a durable layer (task-tree, decision record, knowledge card) and committed —
never carried only in the conversation or in the resume pointer.

## Consequences

- A director ruling delivered mid-session is recorded before the turn ends (the
  `2026-10-02` apparatus and MEMORY rulings were: tree + decision records in the same session).
- The resume pointer quotes rulings by reference, never as their only home — which is what
  lets `MEMORY.md` stay a bounded pointer.
