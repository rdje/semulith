## The fact / decision

The repo-local workflow decides the four items and owns the decisions. Each ruling is its own record
here, each is executed by a task-tree leaf under the normal gated commit, and each names what it rests on
so the director can audit it without the conversation:
[`decision_check-examples-stays-in-the-cli.md`](decision_check-examples-stays-in-the-cli.md),

## Why

The items had waited on the director for one to three days with their options measured and written down;
the director's delegation makes them the workflow's, under the standard stated. A delegated ruling is
not a lighter ruling: it is recorded with the same authority chain (this record), the same evidence and
the same gate as any other.

## How to apply

- A record's `authority` under this delegation is `review`, never `director`; the record cites this
  delegation. The director may supersede any of the four by a later ruling.
- A future open item of the same kind (a design choice the measurements already decide) is decided by the
  workflow under this standard and recorded; an item that changes a profile's claims, a retained case or
  a policy still goes to the director first ([[raise-blockers-early-and-loud]]).
