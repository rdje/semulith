## What "accepted" means here

1. **The gate passed over exact bytes.** Each axis was measured from tracked inputs, and each recorded verdict states what it covers. A change to the engine, the definitions or the guests lapses the rows it touches, and a change to the dossier lapses this decision.
2. **It is finite evidence** (`EVD-01`). The model agrees with the pinned reference on the declared observations of the programs the evidence names, and nothing more. Each credit keeps its own limits.
3. **It authorizes the next stage.** `ROADMAP.md`: this gate "authorizes the planned next engineering stage: board implementation".
4. **It is not a conformance claim.** RISC-V International has certified nothing here, and "supports RV64GC" appears nowhere.

## The capability limits

`gate.sexp` declares them as `limit` forms and the report lists them. In short: one hart; no board, no boot, no multicore and no weak-memory exploration; one pinned reference whose ancestry is unexamined; `wfi`'s stall and injected suboperation faults are uncredited; the ABI statement holds for what the pinned compiler builds; and everything the next pinned window carries ([`decision_csr-write-three-producers.md`](decision_csr-write-three-producers.md)).
