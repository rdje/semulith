# Disposition of the v0.1 review

> **Orientation.** What happened to each finding `M1`–`M19` of the review of roadmap v0.1:
> accepted, adapted, or rejected, with the v0.2 treatment named.
>
> It is kept in the book because a plan that silently absorbs its review is indistinguishable
> from a plan that ignored it. Three entries are worth noting:
>
> - **`M1` rejected** as a forced either/or, while its underlying point — clearer priorities —
>   was accepted. Validated processors are the first product; boards and computers are
>   subsequent products.
> - **`M4` adapted**: early oracle acquisition accepted, but the proposed universal two-tier
>   grading replaced by capability-specific obligations plus recorded ancestry. A single ranking
>   over incomparable evidence types is a summary that destroys the distinction it summarises.
> - **`M9` corrected**: finite differential equality is not universal proof, and producing only
>   legal executions is not the same as exploring all permitted ones.
>
> The review's unsourced 10× / 70% effort estimates and its assumptions about mandatory human
> review hours were **not adopted**. Estimates follow measured experiments.
