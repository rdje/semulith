# Sources and naming

> **Orientation.** The reviewed source list, with an explicit boundary on every row, and the
> naming position.
>
> The boundary matters more than the list. These links are *discovery inputs*: they are not a
> dependency lockfile, and they are not proof that a tool has been acquired, built, or
> qualified for our selected profile. P0 records the exact applicable revisions, commits,
> configurations, artifacts, and terms — and source accessibility never establishes permission
> to redistribute a source, a binary, or a derived artifact.
>
> No Arm reference model, production Rust floating-point library, DSP oracle, or physical
> target is selected or qualified by this list.
>
> On the name: **Semulith** is a recommendation. A preliminary indexed search found no obvious
> exact software-project collision, and a direct registry lookup **did not establish
> availability**. Nothing has been reserved. No engineering choice depends on the final name.
