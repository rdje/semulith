## Existing declarations are inputs, not automatic conversions

The current TSV has destination, route class, lifecycle, owner, two health
cells and three ceiling cells. For files, its line axis is LF lines; for
families it is file count. Bytes are per-file or family aggregate, and the
final cell bounds an authored member. These overloaded axes must be expanded
explicitly into the neutral six-dimensional measurement:
`files`, `lines_each`, `bytes_each`, `line_bytes_each`, `lines_total`,
`bytes_total`. Zero means ungoverned locally; it is not a neutral positive
budget or evidence of health. Preserve all current enforcement until an owned
replacement is qualified, including checked derived-member exemptions.

| Local label | Required review before neutral declaration |
| --- | --- |
| `hot_live` | Actual audience, overwrite semantics, bounded snapshot and current-state fields |
| `partitioned` | Separate product reference, work records and projections; index completeness and navigation cost |
| `generated_index` | Canonical inputs, executed freshness proof and generated locator |
| `append_history` | Whole-entry ledger, bounded live window, complete partition and archive transition |
| `frozen` | Exact retained capture, consumers, retrieval and frozen-legacy versus archive semantics |
| `normative` | Authoritative contract, amendment rules, reader cost and explicit bounded/reference classification |
| `archive_terminal` | Exact immutable members, retrieval proof, metadata bounds and retention guarantee |

Neither a filename extension nor its date, age or number-shaped prose chooses
a lifecycle. A technical manual that is uniquely useful is not obsolete
because it grows. Review audience, utility, authority and actual consumers
before choosing bounded snapshot, partitioned canonical, maintained reference,
generated projection, rolling ledger, archive terminal, external terminal or
frozen legacy. Record why other plausible classes were rejected.

## `.4i2`: discover and close the complete graph

1. Pin a fresh immutable source and the real HEAD/index/working identities.
   Start with every tracked path, bootstrap, live index, registry, book source,
   workflow, tool catalogue and documented author entrypoint. Enumerate owned
   untracked additions separately. Treat Gitlinks as opaque published contracts.
2. Extract reader candidates from literal Markdown links, mdBook includes and
   SUMMARY files, index/query expansion, generated navigation and documented
   retrieval commands. Resolve includes after their actual expansion rules;
   record source path, exact marker, target, kind and consumer. Walk every newly
   reached surface until the frontier is empty, retaining visited identities.
3. Run existing enforcers with deliberate pressure/input failures in isolated
   repository-volume fixtures. Capture their actual emitted overflow hints and
   compare those author candidates with declared routes. Reading source strings
   alone cannot establish emitted author-route coverage.
4. Distinguish pressure/navigation edges from citations, backlinks and links
   internal to one collection. Account for every candidate and justify each
   exclusion. Do not discard an edge merely to avoid a cycle: resolve the actual
   reader/author contract and reject unresolved cycles in the declared graph.
5. Expand every collection, generated view and query into exact members and
   canonical inputs. Identify bounded indexes and navigation depth; enumerate
   archive/external/frozen terminals and their authenticated retrieval path.
   Record overlaps and distinguish ownership from intentional multiple views.
6. Produce a closed candidate ledger with a surface or reasoned exclusion for
   every member and route candidate. Reconcile it independently with the source
   inventory and emitted-hint census. Qualification must detect an omitted
   member, route, include expansion and terminal; a registry self-count is not
   independent coverage evidence.

The deliverable records consumers and evidence, plus undecided utility or
lifecycle questions for `.4i3`; it does not settle those by scanner heuristics.
Ignored caches and generated outputs still need locality and regeneration
dispositions where they participate in a reader or author route. Read-only
operating-system/toolchain dependencies need an identified necessity, not a
project-owned off-volume store.

## `.4i3–4`: decisions, fields and pressure

For each surface record audience, purpose, utility, canonical authority,
consumers, locator, exact targets, index contract and outgoing routes. Review
rejected lifecycle choices and retention constraints. A lifecycle change that
changes direct browsing needs its required director decision before migration.

Declare derived current-state fields individually with exact literal markers,
canonical authority, derivation and executed verifier, or derive on read with
the stored field absent. Do not infer currency from dates or numbers. A valid
empty field registry is preferable to invented fields; demonstrate its reviewed
coverage. Canonical product facts and immutable historical receipts retain
their separate meanings.

Measure all six dimensions after approved trimming or partitioning. Separate
health targets, enforcement ceilings, warning/rollover milestones and exact
debt baselines. Existing task health64 remains warning debt despite count107;
the count application did not adopt the full doctrine. A debt transition needs
an owner, finite growth allowance and ratchet; recording occupancy cannot
declare it healthy or renew the ceiling. Maintained-reference aggregate change
needs independently checked prior baseline, signed delta and fresh authority;
per-part and mandatory-reading/navigation bounds remain fixed. Do not copy
another project's paths, thresholds, owners or baseline debt.

## `.4i5–6`: qualify and integrate the checker

Locally own the reviewed neutral checker before use. Its six JSONL control
registries cover surfaces, derived state, routes, evidence maps, archives and
ledger manifests; retention contracts are an additional required input when
version-object retention is used. Every registry has exactly one schema-v1
metadata record with reviewed positive record, byte and per-record limits.
Blank/comment lines, unknown keys, duplicate IDs, unbounded scalar/array data,
unsafe paths and incomplete references refuse. Derive local control-plane
bounds after the inventory; portable fail-safe maxima are not local budgets.

Execute every declared core verifier and adapter. Emit exact surface/archive/
currency/derived proof tokens only after successful execution; missing,
duplicate or unused tokens refuse. External degraded evidence cannot make a
green local verdict. Prove index membership and generated freshness, exact
archive identity/retrieval and ledger prefix/order/source reconstruction.

The neutral checker reads the resulting tree; local revision-aware adapters
must separately check fresh capacity/reference authority, exact baseline and
delta, no reused or banked authorization, and no silent lifecycle loss. Pass a
checked NUL candidate stream to coverage, and independently derive author
candidates from emitted hints so deleting a declaration cannot hide one.
Require controlled staged inputs to equal the verified working inputs before
committing. Add unconditional driver/hook/CI execution and mirror its contract.
Keep existing guards until the replacement proves their duties. Do not freeze
live cardinalities into integration tests; fixed synthetic fixtures may assert
their own exact counts.

## `.4i7–8`: migration and signoff

Give each measured migration its own atomic task leaf and commit. Preserve
whole original bytes, ordering, modes, indexes and incoming section targets;
qualify reconstruction and deliberate corruption/omission refusals. Retention
must work under the promised clone conditions; Git ancestry alone is not an
independently demonstrated archive guarantee. Do not delete unique material or
extend a finite sealed capture. Validate complete coverage, utility decisions,
pressure/debt, fields, retrieval and rendered mdBooks before full adoption is
claimed. Until then, `LIVE-CONTAINMENT.4` remains active and P4 adequacy waits.
